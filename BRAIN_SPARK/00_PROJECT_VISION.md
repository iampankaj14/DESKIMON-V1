# 00 — PROJECT VISION

## What is SPARK (Deskimon)?

Deskimon / SPARK is a physical ESP32-S3-based desktop companion with a round touch display that shows animated eyes and expressions. It listens to voice, understands intent, and responds with personality through a cloud-connected AI pipeline — all in under 2 seconds.

It is NOT a smart speaker. It is NOT a productivity app. It is a **character** that lives on your desk.

---

## Why Does It Exist?

Standard smart speakers are faceless, transactional, and cold. Deskimon exists to be something different:
- A companion that reacts to how you feel
- A presence that has genuine personality (not corporate friendliness)
- A device that watches your desk habits and has opinions about them
- A physical object that bridges AI conversation with physical expression

---

## Long-Term Vision

> "Turn Deskimon from a voice receiver into an autonomous desk agent with permanent memory, workspace awareness, and a personality that grows with the user."

**Phase milestones toward this vision:**

| Phase | Goal |
|-------|------|
| V1 | Sub-2-second voice responses, 26 face expressions, stable firmware |
| V2 | Offline wake word, dynamic face themes, neural personality engine |
| V3 | Desktop app integration (reads active apps, calendar, build logs) |
| V4 | Long-term vector memory, autonomous reactions, persistent identity |

---

## Product Philosophy

1. **Character over capability** — Personality is the differentiator, not features.
2. **Speed is personality** — A slow response breaks the illusion. Sub-2s is non-negotiable.
3. **Expressions are emotions** — Every AI response maps to a visual face state.
4. **Offline-first intent, cloud-fallback intelligence** — Local intent engine intercepts ~45% of queries before they hit any API.
5. **The device must feel alive** — Micro-animations, idle drift, blink cycles, and IMU reactions keep the face "breathing" even during silence.
6. **No corporate AI vibes** — Spark never says "How can I help you today?" or "I'm sorry, I don't understand."
